# Avera

Avera is a WhatsApp-native, non-custodial crypto payment infrastructure designed to simplify blockchain payments through conversational interfaces while expanding real-world accessibility of decentralized finance systems.

The platform enables users to send and receive digital assets directly inside WhatsApp using simple natural language commands such as:

> “Send 5 USDC to Daniel.”

Instead of requiring traditional wallet applications, complex onboarding flows, or long wallet addresses, Avera abstracts blockchain complexity behind a familiar messaging experience.

The project is designed as a consumer-focused payment layer capable of supporting future integrations with the 0 ecosystem, Soroban smart contracts, and cross-chain payment infrastructure.

---

# 🌍 Why Avera Matters

One of the biggest barriers to blockchain adoption is usability.

Most users:
- do not understand wallets
- struggle with addresses and seed phrases
- avoid complex interfaces
- cannot easily onboard into decentralized finance systems

Avera addresses this problem by turning messaging platforms into payment interfaces.

By allowing users to transact through natural language conversations, Avera helps make decentralized payments:
- more accessible
- easier to understand
- mobile-native
- socially integrated
- suitable for emerging markets and mainstream adoption

# 🧩 How It Works

## 1. User Starts a Conversation

The user opens WhatsApp and chats with the Avera bot.

---

## 2. Wallet Onboarding

Avera connects the user’s messaging identity to a non-custodial wallet infrastructure.

Future ecosystem integrations may support:
- Stellar wallets
- Soroban-compatible identity systems
- decentralized account abstraction patterns

---

## 3. Natural Language Payment

Users can initiate payments using simple conversational prompts such as:

> “Pay John 20 USDC for the design project.”

This removes the complexity of traditional crypto interfaces.

---

## 4. Identity & Trust Verification

The system verifies:
- sender authenticity
- receiver trust
- transaction legitimacy

The trust layer is designed to support future decentralized verification and programmable trust systems.

---

## 5. On-Chain Execution

The backend:
- constructs transactions
- broadcasts them on-chain
- monitors confirmation status
- returns payment receipts

---

## 6. Conversational Confirmation

Both sender and receiver receive:
- clean transaction receipts
- payment confirmations
- transaction status updates directly in WhatsApp
